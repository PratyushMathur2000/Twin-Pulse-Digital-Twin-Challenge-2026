Biophysical ODE Engine

Multi-compartment differential equations model glucose-insulin dynamics (Bergman Minimal Model), autonomic baroreflex feedback, systemic vascular resistance, and glomerular ultrafiltration.

  • • Numerical Solver: 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) with adaptive step size (Δt = 0.05 h).
  • • Coupling Matrices: Symmetric feedback between autonomic vagal tone, hepatic gluconeogenesis, and renal vascular resistance.
  • • Calibration: Individualized patient baseline tuning with parameter scaling calibrated against holdout windows.

3-Stage ML Prognostic Core

Validation Methodology & Clinical Limitations Statement

Synthetic Cohort Calibration:

The digital twin models were trained on 100 longitudinal synthetic patient records generated via Synthea and physiologically calibrated against epidemiological distributions from the ICMR-INDIAB study. Continuous telemetry windows were generated using Runge-Kutta numerical integration with Gaussian noise.

Prospective Clinical Trial Roadmap:

This software is intended as an investigational Clinical Decision Support (CDS) prototype for the Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026. Prior to real-world clinical deployment, prospective observational clinical trials with live continuous glucose monitors (Dexcom G7, Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3) and ambulatory blood pressure monitors are required.
